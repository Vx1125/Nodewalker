# Changelog

## 1.1.0

- Recipes: every recipe in the game, with vendors, drops and quests. Search by recipe, material, mob or zone.
- Level to slider: stop the plan and shopping list at any skill.
- Train rows: where to learn Expert and Artisan, with a map pin on click.
- Plans count trainer fees and add a few spare crafts per step.
- Plans use recipes from the skill they're learned at, not from yellow.
- Shopping list: recipes to buy or find are listed and priced.
- Shopping list: click an item to search Auctionator for what you still need.
- Profession window button matches the game's own tabs.
- Known recipes are kept per character.
- Lighter overlay with many saved spots.
- Fewer chat messages.
- Fixed errors on the Fishing page and with nameplates.
- Licence: GPL-3.0-or-later. Recipe data derived from One For All.

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Map overlay: live minimap terrain, herb and ore icons, live dots, range ring.
- Routes: optimized loops, avoid zones, herb routes, auto-routes in new zones.
- Herb Guide: best zones per herb, ranked from your own node data.
- Professions: leveling plans for every profession, priced from Auctionator.
- Shopping list, missing recipes with sources, profession window button.
- GatherMate2 import.
