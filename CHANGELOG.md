# Changelog

## 1.1.7

- Craft button on Leveling and Profit rows and on recipe cards: makes as many as your bags allow. The first click opens the profession.
- Profit: Have materials filter.
- Professions: labelled cost and columns, plainer option names, a key under the shopping list.
- "Recipes" is now "Missing recipes"; a profession at 300 opens on Profit.
- Consumes: alternatives show as "or" and are stocked once (Winterfall or Distilled Firewater, flasks, food, oils).
- Consumes: runes, rogue poisons, Lobster Stew, Smoked Desert Dumplings; corrected effect numbers.
- Fixed shift-click on a recipe linking to chat with the auction house open.

## 1.1.6

- Consumes: lists for every class and spec, with a Budget list of cheaper tiers.
- Consumes: Stock for a 2, 3 or 4 hour raid, Make and Buy columns, recipe known or where to get it.
- Consumes: one search for everything you're short of; Farm button; right-click to hide a row.
- Farm Guide (was Herb Guide): ore list, a Both view for Herbalism + Mining, and gold per hour.
- Profit counts disenchant value when it pays more than selling.
- Shift-click always searches at the auction house.
- Fixed overlapping text in the profession header and the Herbs & Ore list.
- `/nw consumes`.

## 1.1.5

- New Consumes tab: raid consumables for Holy Paladin and Warlock, with stock, prices and materials.
- Leveling: Crafts dropdown (Keep, Sell, Disenchant) plans around what you do with the items.
- Leveling cost is what you pay; sale or disenchant value is shown beside it.
- Recipes view: click a recipe for its materials; Alt-click searches the recipe.
- Shift-click a recipe at the auction house searches what it makes.

## 1.1.4

- Profit view: click a recipe to see its materials and what each costs.
- Material tooltips show auction, vendor and crafting prices.
- Click a craftable material to see how to make it.
- Alt-click a recipe to search the auction house for what it makes.

## 1.1.3

- Professions: Leveling, Recipes and Profit are tabs under the profession's name.
- Profit view: what each craft earns at today's prices, best first.
- Recipes view grouped by the game's categories (Stamina Food, Attack Power Food...).

## 1.1.2

- Overlay: spots that overlap share one icon with a count.
- Overlay: route line has a dark edge, fades with distance and shows direction arrows.
- Overlay: picked stops shrink to a small dot; quieter dashed range ring.
- Overlay: Muted and Silhouette terrain colours.
- Route page: loop coloured gold to blue from your next stop, finer on busy routes, hover a stop for details.
- Professions: Shift-click a recipe or shopping item to link it in chat.
- Fixed: errors every frame with Hide in combat off.
- Fixed: quest objective tooltips popping up over the overlay.

## 1.1.1

- Minimap button: click for the overlay, right-click for the window.
- Session panel: value gathered and gold per hour.
- Overlay presets: Farming, Questing, Minimal.
- Zoom out when mounted; fading eases and lifts near a node.
- Lighter overlay: icons and route glide with the map.

- Fixed: Count sale value made the plan pick dozens of recipes.

## 1.1.0

- Recipes: every recipe in the game, with vendors, drops and quests. Search by recipe, material, mob or zone.
- Level to slider: stop the plan and shopping list at any skill.
- Train rows: where to learn Expert and Artisan, with a map pin on click.
- Plans count trainer fees and add a few spare crafts per step.
- Plans use recipes from the skill they're learned at, not from yellow.
- Shopping list: recipes to buy or find are listed and priced.
- Shopping list: click an item to search Auctionator for what you still need.
- Profession window button matches the game's own tabs.
- Known recipes are kept per character.
- Lighter overlay with many saved spots.
- Fewer chat messages.
- Fixed errors on the Fishing page and with nameplates.

## 1.0.0

- First release.
- Map overlay: live minimap terrain, herb and ore icons, live dots, range ring.
- Routes: optimized loops, avoid zones, herb routes, auto-routes in new zones.
- Herb Guide: best zones per herb, ranked from your own node data.
- Professions: leveling plans for every profession, priced from Auctionator.
- Shopping list, missing recipes with sources, profession window button.
- GatherMate2 import.
