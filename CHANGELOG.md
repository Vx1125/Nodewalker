# Changelog

## Unreleased

- Route tab merged into Material Finder: Routes button, saved routes picker, the same editor and Expand.
- Material Finder: Expand on a zone's route opens the full-size route editor; Edit route opens its saved route.
- GatherMate2 import moved to Settings › Routes.
- Routes: clicking the Material Finder tab goes back to materials.
- Material Finder: clearer wording for the ways to get a crafted item.
- Leveling: step ranges stay as planned instead of moving up with your skill.
- Shopping list: enough spare materials for bad luck, so one trip covers the plan.
- Re-plan moved to Options: Re-plan at today's prices.
- Leveling: a recipe is made until it turns green, then the plan moves to one that's still orange or yellow.
- Route tab: Start keeps the overlay as you have it.
- Material Finder: smoother scrolling; recipe view wider, without repeated material lines.
- Leveling: tools get their own row when first needed (Blacksmith Hammer, Arclight Spanner, Gyromatic Micro-Adjustor, enchanting rods) and go on the shopping list.
- Leveling: a recipe that comes back after other steps does the points between when it costs about the same; plans are chosen again once.
- Leveling: parts you make first (Handful of Copper Bolts, blasting powder) get their own row before the step.
- Routes are reviewed in the Route tab: Material Finder route buttons open a proposal; nothing is replaced until Start.
- Hunting grounds list every creature with spawn points (Heavy Leather: 204 skinnable, was 80).
- Spawn points 10× more precise; dense camps no longer merged.
- Drop chances under 1% shown (e.g. 0.2%); calculated chances say so.
- Material Finder reads the full recipe catalog: more vendor and crafted materials.
- Light Leather and Rugged Leather show Crafting as a source.
- Stonescale Eel added to the fishing data.
- Memory: hunting grounds kept for the 4 most recent views.
- `/nw perf`: memory, caches and timings (developer).

## 1.2.0

- Material Finder: one search for every material (herbs, ore, leather, fish, cloth, meat, reagents, vendor goods, crafted parts).
- Material Finder: category and filters (in bags, priced, can gather), sort by name, price or owned.
- Material Finder: larger map with known spots, your history, heatmap and routes; zone list beside it.
- Hunting grounds for skins and creature drops: click a ground or creature, farm one area, edit the route.
- Targets: track how many of a material you want; the session panel shows progress.
- Gathering log: what you skinned, looted and gathered, and where.
- Fish Finder is now the Fish category of the Material Finder.
- Leveling kits: each profession keeps one 1-300 plan; Re-plan picks new recipes at today's prices.
- Leveling: plans make their own bolts and bars when cheaper, and stop hopping between recipes.
- Leveling: shopping lists count bolts already in your bags and bolts made along the way.
- Leveling: no more riding a recipe to grey; cooldown materials only as a last resort.
- Leveling: recipe colours and trainer recipes are learned from your profession and trainer windows.
- Recipe data checked against WoW Forever sources for every profession.
- Tabs: Professions, Material Finder, Route, Find a craft, Consumes, Settings.
