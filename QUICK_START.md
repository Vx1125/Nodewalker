# Nodewalker quick start

Type `/nw prof` to open the window, or click the Nodewalker button on your minimap or in the AddOns menu.

## First time

1. Install **Auctionator** and scan the auction house once. That gives you prices.
2. Optional: install **GatherMate2** and **GatherMate2_Data**, log in, and type `/nw import`. That gives you thousands of known herb and ore spots at once. You can turn GatherMate2 off afterwards.

## Start gathering

1. Type `/nw guide` (Farm Guide).
2. Pick an herb or ore on the left.
3. Click a zone in the list. The map shows its loop.
4. Click **Build route**.
5. Go to that zone and type `/nw` to show the overlay.
6. Follow the cyan line. Green markers are on your minimap right now; faded ones are spots seen before.

When you enter a zone without a route, Nodewalker builds one by itself. To rebuild a route or steer it away from somewhere, open the Route tab (`/nw plan`), then use **Optimize loop** or **Draw avoid zone**.

## Level a profession

1. Type `/nw prof`.
2. Pick a profession on the left.
3. Open **Leveling**.
4. Set **Target skill** and a **Strategy** (Cheapest, Fewest crafts, Known recipes).
5. Read the plan: steps by rank, crafts per step, total cost.
6. At the auction house, click **Search missing** and buy what's listed.
7. Visit each trainer as the plan reaches it (click a Train row to put the trainer on your map), and craft from the plan.

Costs and craft counts are estimates. Prices move and skill-ups are random.

## Find crafts worth making

1. Type `/nw prof` and pick a profession.
2. Open **Profit**.
3. Sort by **Profit each**, **Return** or **Total I can make**.
4. Click a recipe to see its materials and what each costs.

Profit uses current listings after the auction cut. A listing isn't a sale.

## Level Fishing and Cooking together

1. Type `/nw prof` and pick **Fishing** (or Cooking, then **Fishing + Cooking plan**).
2. Open **Fishing + Cooking** and set a target for each.
3. Do the step with the gold arrow. Fish counts update as you catch and cook.

## Stock up for a raid

1. Type `/nw consumes`.
2. Pick your class and spec, or **My level** while leveling.
3. Pick how long the raid is.
4. Click **Search missing** to find everything you're short of.
