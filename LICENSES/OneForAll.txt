One For All states that this data comes from:
  - the WoW Forever client database (build 1.60.1.70205), via wago.tools DB2 exports: names, items,
    reagents, counts, skill colours, recipe items;
  - QuestieDB and Questie (GPL-3.0): recipe sources such as vendors, drops and quests;
  - CMaNGOS classic-db (GPL-3.0): trainer fees, ranks and required skill (npc_trainer);
  - ProfessionDB / LibProfessionDB (MIT, below): which recipes are Merchant's Favor recipes.
One For All notes that some learn levels (marked rw=true) come from wowtbc.gg and that some recipe
sources come from Wowhead Forever. Nodewalker leaves the rw=true learn levels out (unknown instead);
a Classic trainer level (CMaNGOS) is used only where Forever's skill colours agree with it.
Mining.lua: bars, reagents, counts and learn levels from Data/Smelting.lua (Forever client data). Which
bars a trainer teaches comes from cMaNGOS classic-db (GPL-3.0, npc_trainer and spell_template at
commit 13e933726f38, see cMaNGOS-classic-db.txt), as Classic reference data. Skill-up colours come
from Warcraft Wiki (CC BY-SA 4.0) and live in their own file, Professions/SmeltingColours.lua (see
Warcraft-Wiki-CC-BY-SA-4.0.txt).
