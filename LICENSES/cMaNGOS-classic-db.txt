(https://github.com/cmangos/classic-db), released under the GNU General Public License
version 3. Pinned commit: 13e933726f386399d9820138fbd1123e7cda86c8 (2026-11-21).

What was used, and how it was changed (tools/build_sources.py):
  - creature_template: level range, creature type, rank, and the skinning loot id
    (also used as the skinnable flag), and CreatureTypeFlags: creatures flagged for
    Herbalism, Mining or Engineering (0x100, 0x200, 0x8000) and critters (type 8) are not
    counted as skinning sources. Names come from pfQuest where it has one.
    Faction and respawn time come from pfQuest.
  - skinning_loot_template: skinning chances and quantities. Group entries with chance 0
    are given their calculated share of the group, and are listed in S.calc.
  - reference_loot_template: chances for loot that pfQuest lists as reference loot,
    multiplied through the reference. These are also listed in S.calc.
  - fishing_loot_template (reference tables 11009 and 11105) and the Stonescale Eel Swarm
    gameobject (180712): looked up by hand to add Stonescale Eel's zones (Tanaris, Azshara) to
    Professions/Fishing.lua. Only the zone names were used; no chance was copied.
  - Changes: records were filtered to the configured materials and to open-world creatures
    with spawn points. Every dropped record is listed with a reason in
    tools/reports/rejected_sources.csv. Values were rounded for size: chances of 1% or more
    to 0.01, smaller ones to 2 significant figures. Spawn points come from pfQuest (below).

Creature spawn points, ordinary loot chances and vendors come from pfQuest (MIT, Shagu);
see LICENSES/pfQuest-MIT.txt.
